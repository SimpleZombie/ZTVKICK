# ZTV: product facts (for the guide redesign)

## Who and what
- **Viewers** on kick.com/simplezombie. They mostly watch passively, often in Kick's small player on a phone or in a side tab. Their question: *what is this, what's on, and is anything better on?*
- **Chat** surfs with !ch, !guide, requests and votes. Their question: *which channel number do I type?*
- **Cameron** runs it. His questions: *does it look like ZTV, and does it stay readable when Kick scales it down?*

## Verified facts
- The page is 1280x720, captured by ffmpeg and streamed at about 1.8 Mbps. Small text and fine lines blur at that bitrate.
- 63 channels of old TV from archive.org, each with a pixel-art CRT icon Cameron picked ("Much better" over toony and line icons).
- The guide grid scrolls through every channel in a loop. The tuned channel's row is often off screen.
- The video window is about 37% of the width. The info panel and guide take the rest.
- Mixed type today: Oswald (headings), VT323 (body), Arial (grid). The palette is olive and acid green, with a purple late-night mode.
- Takeovers, bumper cards and video bumpers share the green, cream and near-black look from the Nov 10 style pass.
- Rejected before: the "Watch view" layout changes (Nov), every logo round except 3C (on hold), toony or line icons, and the Alamo/VHS yellow theme (Nov 20).
- Kept on purpose: the ZTV mark, the cable/VHS feel, all chat commands and key bindings.

## What a mistake costs
- Unreadable when scaled down: viewers can't tell what's on and leave.
- Losing the tuned channel in the grid: chat can't find the number.
- Restyling without asking: another rejected round.

## Gaps
- No data on how big most viewers' players are, or on whether people watch the video or the guide more.
